# ZICOFE Website — Stakeholder Update

**Reporting period:** 21–30 October 2026  
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026

Recent work has focused on improving the website’s appearance, updating company information, making it easier for customers to contact the team, and improving the coffee order enquiry process.

The changes below have been prepared. Their availability on the public website has not yet been confirmed. **Order enquiries still need a successful email delivery test before the online ordering process can be considered ready.**

## A more consistent brand presentation

- Refined the ZICOFE logo at the top of the website, including its size and spacing on different screen sizes.
- Updated the logo at the bottom of the website to suit the dark background.
- Reorganised the bottom section so visitors can more easily find company links, contact details, certifications, and privacy information.
- Improved spacing and added gold accents to match the brand.

These changes aim to make the website clearer to navigate and more consistent in its presentation.

## Updated company information and photographs

- Updated the company introduction to say **“42 Years of Excellence.”**
- Updated photographs of the farm, awards, and Mr. Enock Ssenyonga on the About page.
- Updated a photograph illustrating natural coffee processing.
- Added links to articles about Women In Coffee and Youth Empowerment, allowing visitors to read more about these initiatives.

## Easier ways to contact ZICOFE

The Contact Us section now presents four clear options: **Email, Call, WhatsApp, and Visit us**. Customers can select an option to open their email app, make a call, start a WhatsApp conversation, or get directions to the roastery.

## Improved Shop Now enquiries

The Shop Now form is intended to let customers request a quotation for their coffee requirements. It does not take payment or confirm a purchase.

Recent improvements include:

- Corrected a selection issue that could prevent customers from submitting the form.
- Added checks for missing or invalid order and contact details.
- Kept the customer’s entries on screen if submission fails, so they do not have to start again.
- Added clearer messages and alternative email and WhatsApp contact options when an enquiry cannot be sent.
- Changed the email delivery service to **FormSubmit**, which requires simpler setup.

Order notifications are configured to go to **silva@zicofe.com**. They include the customer’s contact information, coffee type and grade, quantity, delivery address, and any additional notes. The team can reply to the notification to contact the customer.
